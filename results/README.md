# Frozen result artifacts

- `frozen_json/motion_margin_final_natural`: four unmodified receiver panels.
- `frozen_json/motion_margin_final_fault`: seven synchronized-common-epoch
  controlled receiver-fault cases on the Medium route.
- `paper_summaries/motion_margin_final_statistics`: principal paired statistics.
- `paper_summaries/motion_margin_ci_statistics_final`: CI comparisons.
- `paper_summaries/motion_margin_final_ablation_statistics`: decision ablations.
- `paper_summaries/motion_margin_fault_severity_statistics`: magnitude/onset sweep.
- `paper_summaries/motion_margin_leave_one_panel_out`: frozen-parameter audit.
- `runtime_repeats`: five HK Medium timing runs.
- `revision_audit`: same-subset CU, factor-calibration, topology-decision and
  route-balanced compact summaries used by the revised manuscript.

These are compact audit outputs, not replacements for the public source
datasets.  Local absolute paths were removed from the published manifests.
The four natural panels and seven stress variants are tested cases, not 11
independent routes.
